pTOS - Portable TOS
===================

pTOS is a portable, open-source TOS-compatible operating system derived from
EmuTOS: https://github.com/emutos/emutos

EmuTOS concentrates on compatibility with existing Atari hardware, compatible
machines, and software.  pTOS uses that foundation to experiment with new
features and architectures while retaining the familiar GEMDOS, BIOS/XBIOS,
VDI, AES, and desktop environment.

The work that brought EmuTOS to Amiga hardware and, especially, ColdFire-based
boards demonstrated that TOS could be useful beyond its original hardware.
That experience led to pTOS's goal of making the operating system portable to
further architectures and machines.

pTOS would not be possible without the substantial work of the EmuTOS
contributors.  Their copyright notices and the project's history are retained
in this source tree.  pTOS is independently maintained, however: bugs in pTOS
are the responsibility of the pTOS team, not the EmuTOS development team.

For the source, build instructions, supported configurations, and issue
tracker, visit:

https://github.com/kelihlodversson/pTOS
