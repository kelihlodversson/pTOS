Creation paths and compatibility decision
-----------------------------------------
Both4/104 authenticate PE_LOAD and reuse its distinct staged KPROC/PD/PID.
The trusted NEW_PROCESS/SHARE_VM factory constructs the authentic initial
KTHREAD with independent kernel stacks. Mode4 waits for final PROCESS completion
and its full16-bit status;104 returns the PID. Both consume #909's canonical
once-only reaper, never SAME_PROCESS or a second registry/scheduler/reaper.

Managed ordinary0/100/6/106 and already-prepared creation use the loaded THREAD
factory. KPROC/environment/TPA allocation and image policy remain in the loader.

adds no duplicate calls. An enclosing carrier keeps its own identity while the
nested logical execution owns its THREAD/cleanup tickets. Unmanaged ROM and
accessory paths preserve their documented behavior. Sole-THREAD nested public
clone/join/group restrictions remain; ordinary managed THREAD coverage does not
claim those operations for nested continuations.

Unannotated Malloc/Mmap uses common GENERAL membership, including existing and
future allocations, so surviving peers keep a departed creator's allocations.
Explicit test-gated MAP_PTOS_RUNTIME annotation selects runtime-owned DSO maps;
loaded execution has a fresh runtime scope and exact source-owned dependencies.
Generic CLONE_VM inherits compatible current/future runtime membership. A raw
shared pointer alone never exports lifetime across runtime scopes. Trusted exact
birth/interval attachment must precede source release. Lifetime is neither
memory access nor immutable permission authority. See shared-vm-pexec.txt for
FreeMiNT primary-source references and the explicit public-activation boundary.

Regression diagnoses retained as evidence
------------------------------------------
8ece5bde leaked interpreter mappings in all five native configurations; that
negative evidence and disposable preview results remain historical, not final.
The m68k nested peer-chain98 and GLOBALCHffff were traced before role entry to
_crtinit_noargs's insufficient-TPA termination. Actual TPAs77,824/61,440 bytes
cannot fit default64KiB stacks plus image/MINFREE; bounded helper frames are
below1KiB, and the helpers explicitly request16KiB. No arbitrary TPA increase,
CRT admission bypass, libcmini modification or lifecycle assertion removal.

The separate multiple-orphan lost-guard panic came from production m68k memmove
copying low chunks first inside backward64/256-byte and odd16-byte overlap.
The correction uses descending chunks/bytes for classic and ColdFire. An actual
kernel-symbol native oracle covers1600 cases including the old failing overlap;
runners reject a kernel panic even if a continuation resumes afterward.

Positive full MEMTEST retains default-off rejection expectations and separately
checks enabled-mode execution/authentication. Failed shared preparation permits
isolated mode6 on the same staged image. Source-PD reuse is forbidden while an
orphan's authentic R1 receipts remain, then required after final dependent THREAD
retirement. The nested cleanup hook authenticates logical owner and depth;
#966's protected destructor can legitimately yield on the restored carrier.
Its observer driver cannot reenter its own in-flight nested/CLOSE scenario.
All once-only completion, enclosing guard/cancellation and final snapshots stay.
